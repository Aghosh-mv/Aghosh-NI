# Novel Techniques for NI

## Implemented ✓

### 1. Spiking Neurons (LIF)
Real neurons use action potentials (spikes), not continuous activations.

### 2. Hebbian Synapses (STDP)
"Neurons that fire together wire together." Synapses strengthen when pre→post.

### 3. Neuromodulation
Dopamine, serotonin, norepinephrine, acetylcholine, GABA, glutamate. Chemical brain state.

### 4. Oscillations
Gamma/theta/alpha/beta/delta waves with cross-frequency coupling. Brain timing.

### 5. Emotional Tagging
Every experience gets emotional tags (fear, reward, novelty, curiosity). Determines what's worth remembering.

### 6. Memory Systems
Hippocampus (episodic), Neocortex (semantic), Cerebellum (procedural), Working Memory.

### 7. Attention Gating
Thalamus as attention router. Bottom-up vs top-down attention competition.

### 8. Predictive Coding
Brain predicts what it expects, only processes SURPRISE (prediction error). Not next-token prediction - prediction of REALITY.

### 9. Exploratory Learning
Trial and error through world. Brain discovers tools, concepts, relationships through experience.
